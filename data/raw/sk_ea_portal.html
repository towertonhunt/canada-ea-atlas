1. Environmental Protection Services

The Environmental Protection Branch handles submissions for environmentally impacted sites, hazardous materials storage facilities, and permitted industrial, mining, and solid waste (landfill) facilities. Please click the Environmental Protection Application Form button below, to start your submission.

The form will direct you to enter applicant information and information about your submission, to help us direct your work to the appropriate Environmental Protection Branch department. The upload section of the form accepts a variety of file types up to 100MB each. Further instructions are also provided to submit larger files.

These documents may be applicable to your submission:

  • Application for Approval to Construct or Upgrade and Operate a HSWDG Storage Facility
  • Application for Approval to Decommission a Hazardous Substances and Waste Dangerous Goods (HSWDG Storage) Facility
  • Corrective Action Plan (CAP)
  • Historical Discovery Report
  • Notice of Site Condition Form
  • Visual Site Assessment (VSA) Checklist
  • 30 Day Written Discharge Report Form

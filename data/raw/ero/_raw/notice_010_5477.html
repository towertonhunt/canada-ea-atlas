Decision summary

The application to amend the Niagara Escarpment Plan to change the designations and/or policies for lands affected by the Escarpment Recreation Area Designation has been closed.

Site location details

Effects of consultation

There was some support for the amendment, however municipalities and landowners;expressed concern with the proposed amendment. They were concerned about the mapping of the Niagara Escarpment's brow and toe, and that development on the Escarpment slope would be limited by the proposed amendment. There were also some objections to the proposed re-designation of some areas from Escarpment Recreation Area to Escarpment Protection and Natural Areas.  

In total, 20 comments were received from agencies, stakeholder groups, and landowners.

No comments were received through the Environmental Registry.

Proposal details

Updates

The notice was updated on January 21, 2016 to advise the public that there has been no change to the status of this proposal and it is still being considered. No additional opportunity for comment is being provided for at this time.

 

Description of instrument

To amend the NEP to change the designations and/or policies respecting those lands affected by the Escarpment Recreation Area Designation in the NEP.

A number of housekeeping matters related to the new mapping and policies will also be addressed as part of the amendment.

Other information

Only Maps 5 and 6 of the NEP are affected by the proposed Designation changes. This includes the Osler Bluff area in Clearview Township (Map 5), and The Town of the Blue Mountains and Municipality of Grey Highlands (Map 6).

Other public consultation opportunities

The Niagara Escarpment Commission mailed requests for comments on this amendment to affected ministries, municipalities, agencies, as well as property owners known to have an interest in the lands which are the subject of this amendment. Notice of this amendment will be placed in the Caledon Citizen, Orangeville Banner, Collingwood Enterprise-Bulletin, Thornbury Courier–Herald, Owen Sound Sun Times and the Wiarton Echo.

Public comments may be submitted in writing to the contact listed in this notice at the NEC until end of business day, January 33, 2009.

Proposal details

This proposal is for a new Environmental Compliance Approval (industrial sewage works) in support of commercial development, and warehousing and shipping activities; for the installation a stormwater structure designed for the collection, transmission, treatment and disposal of stormwater, from a site located at 1060 Innovation Drive, in the City of Kingston, Ontario.

The proposed stormwater management works include three underground storm chambers for quantity and quality control; discharging towards the Northwest of the property ultimately into the Cataraqui River, within the St. Lawrence River Watershed.
